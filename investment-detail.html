Risks to consider

Market risk
Share prices can rise or fall, including the full amount invested.
Company risk
Competition, execution, demand, and management decisions may affect results.
Concentration risk
A single-company position can expose a portfolio to concentrated losses.
Private-market liquidity
Private shares may be restricted, difficult to value, or hard to resell.

This educational material is not personalized investment advice. It does not assess suitability or recommend a security.
